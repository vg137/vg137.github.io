About
me

I am a theoretical and computational physicist with a research interest in the deep connections between condensed matter physics, topology, and photonics.

Outside my research, I enjoy exploring interdisciplinary questions at the intersection of science, computation, and data analysis, including projects in multiomics and large language models.
