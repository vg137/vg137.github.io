Research
interests

My PhD work sits at the intersection of photonics and condensed matter theory. I study theoretical models and perform numerical simulations to understand how light can behave similarly to electrons in different exotic materials.

My research lies at the intersection of condensed matter physics, photonics, and computation. I study how symmetry and topology constrain band structures, allowed frequencies, and localized modes in periodic optical systems.

I also work on small personal projects outside my primary research. These include exploring multiomics and developing a better understanding of transformers and large language models.
